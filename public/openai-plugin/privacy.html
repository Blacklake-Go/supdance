Supdance for OpenAI — Privacy Policy

Effective November 9, 2026. This notice covers data processed by the Supdance plugin for OpenAI. Zhang Donghe operates the plugin and the Supdance service and is responsible for the plugin's data processing. Blacklake Global LLC is the OpenAI directory publisher. The Supdance service is covered by the Supdance privacy policy.

What the plugin processes

  • When you connect an account, the plugin checks the account identifier, the permissions you granted, and token validity. Its web workbench stores its session token encrypted on the server.
  • When you ask for creative assistance, it processes the prompt and any context you submit. The Supdance model gateway receives the inputs needed to produce the requested output. The plugin returns tool results to the OpenAI host you are using.
  • When you request project or canvas tools, it reads the relevant information within your Supdance permissions. It records operation requests, approvals, and receipts so it can confirm outcomes and avoid executing the same request twice.
  • The web workbench stores conversations and drafts. The plugin also records operation times, a hashed account identifier, the action and target, and outcome codes for troubleshooting and accountability.

Controls and privacy requests

You can disconnect the plugin in your OpenAI host to stop future access through that connection. You can delete your conversations in the Supdance plugin workbench. That action does not delete changes previously saved to your Supdance projects or canvases. For access, correction, or deletion requests about plugin records, email support@supdance.com. The service may retain records needed to resolve outstanding operations and meet applicable obligations.

OpenAI explains its handling of data in the OpenAI privacy policy.
